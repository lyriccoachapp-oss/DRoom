Read This Before You Decide

Which stores are in the catalog?

Catalogs are added through retailer partner programs, starting with major home and furniture stores in the US and Canada. Early-access members see the list first.

Do I need design or CAD experience?

No. You give the room and a budget; the planner places the furniture. You only choose what to swap.

Do I need a special phone to measure a room?

No. Corner-tap measuring uses the regular camera on a recent iPhone or Android phone. You can also upload a floor plan or type the room size in.

How accurate is it?

Product sizes come from the store listing. Phone measuring is close enough for furniture; for anything built to fit, check one wall with a tape measure and the plan adjusts to it.

Is this a subscription?

No. One payment covers one home, with no monthly fee.

When does it launch?

The layout engine already works. Plan upload, measuring and store catalogs are in progress, and early-access members get each part as it ships.
